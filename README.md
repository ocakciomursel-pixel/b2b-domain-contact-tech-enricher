> **Bulk Phone Formatter (E.164) & Click-to-WhatsApp Link Generator** — CRM hygiene & WhatsApp sales outreach utility: normalizes phone numbers into E.164 format and builds direct click-to-chat WhatsApp URLs.

- **Direct Structured Extraction**: Engineered with high-speed HTTP and structured API/DOM parsers—zero headless browser overhead and minimal compute cost.
